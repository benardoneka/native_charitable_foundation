About Us

Founded in January 2026 in Kampala, Uganda

Native Charitable Foundation (NCF) is a nonprofit youth-led charity organization based in Kampala, Uganda in East Africa. It was founded in January 2026 by:

MS

Matovu Said

Bakery Chef

SB

Ssemyalo Mukasa Bilal

Electronics Engineer

NCF focuses on supporting underprivileged learning children through our education programs, and hands-on skilling of youths in bakery, beadwork, and crocheting to bridge the gap between imagination and innovation. Through our sanitation program (TWEYONJE Initiative), groups develop skills in briquettes making and improve community hygiene, while we also support needy families and elderly people so they feel honored for helping build our communities.

Our Mission

"To offer hands-on training, financial and material support in education, provide food, improve sanitation, hygiene, healthcare and wellbeing for underprivileged children, youths and elderly people in the communities we serve regardless of the race, age and faith."

Our Vision

"Healthy communities with honored elderly people, schooling children and hands-on skilled youths ready to combat poverty and unemployment to uplift their lives."

Healthcare Campaigns

With assistance from our healthcare partners, we organise medical camps and provide free or low cost medication, checkups or other related services to all the people in our communities.

Emergencies and Relief Aid

We react to emergencies with immediate basic aid such as food, water and medical care supplies to communities affected by natural disasters, conflicts or humanitarian crises to save lives and alleviate suffering.

Fund Emergency Relief →

Food & Nutrition Programs

Most of the families live in extreme poverty that they find it hard to access nutritional foods by themselves. We provide them with uncooked food like rice, posho, beans, cereals and wheat flour.

Provide Food Supplies →
TWEYONJE Initiative

Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WaSH)

We encourage community clean-ups, safe garbage/waste disposal, use of pit latrines and handwashing with soap. This limits the outbreak of disease such as cholera, diarrhea, malaria, hepatitis B and infections in the communities.

Sustainability

How We Raise Funds

Apart from support from donors and sponsors, we generate funds through:

  • Selling branded items featuring the foundation's logo such as t-shirts, sweaters and calendars.
  • Orders for various items through our bakery section from community customers.
  • Collecting money from our fundraising events.
  • Selling craft products on our online crafts store.
  • Financial support from our local leaders.
85% of the funds generated go straight to charity.
